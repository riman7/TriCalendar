Privacy Policy for TriCalendar

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Overview: TriCalendar is built with user privacy as a core principle. Your calendar entries, notes, and events remain stored locally on your device unless you choose to sync with your personal Google Drive account.

1. Information We Collect

TriCalendar does not operate central servers or databases to collect, store, or sell your personal data.

  • Calendar & Event Data: Any events, tithis, notes, or reminders you create are stored locally on your device using local SQLite storage.
  • Account Information: If you connect Google Drive for backup and sync, your Google account email is used strictly for authentication and displaying your active connection status within the app settings.
  • Location Data: TriCalendar may request device location permissions solely for offline celestial / sunrise-sunset calculations and panchang computation. This data never leaves your device.

2. Google Drive Data Access & Usage

TriCalendar offers optional backup and restore functionality powered by Google Drive. When you choose to link your Google account:

  • Limited Scope (drive.file): TriCalendar explicitly requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This grants TriCalendar access only to the files and folders created by TriCalendar itself (such as your backup file tricalendar-backup.json). TriCalendar cannot access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Purpose of Access: The backup file is used solely to store your calendar events and restore them onto your device upon your request.
  • No Third-Party Transfer: We do not transfer, transmit, or share your Google user data or backup files with any third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete any event at any time from within the app, or delete all data by clearing app storage or uninstalling TriCalendar.
  • Google Drive Data: You can remove your backup file at any time directly through Google Drive (in the TriCalendar Backoff folder), or disconnect your Google account inside TriCalendar settings.

4. Children's Privacy

TriCalendar does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
